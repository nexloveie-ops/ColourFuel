Privacy Policy

ColourFuel app and colourfuel.ie · Last updated 20 November 2026

In short: no account, no ads, no tracking. If you choose to send a meal photo, it is used once to identify the foods and is not stored by ColourFuel.

Who we are

ColourFuel, Ireland. Questions about privacy: info@colourfuel.ie.

What we do not collect

  • No sign-up or account: we do not ask for your name, email, phone number or address.
  • No advertising, no analytics SDKs, and no tracking across other apps or websites.
  • No location, contacts or health data.

Photos you choose to send

The photo estimate and "Find by photo" only run when you pick or take a photo and agree to the notice in the app. Then:

  • The app resizes the photo on your device and removes its metadata, such as location and camera details, before sending it over an encrypted connection (HTTPS).
  • Our server, hosted on Google Cloud in the EU (Belgium), passes it to Claude, an AI model from Anthropic, to identify the foods and estimate their weights.
  • ColourFuel does not store or log your photo; it is used for that one request. Anthropic processes it as our service provider under its commercial terms (anthropic.com/legal).
  • Nutrition is then calculated from published food records (USDA FoodData Central, UK CoFID) or a pack label visible in the photo.
  • If a food in your photo is not yet in our ingredient library, we may add that food to the public library: its generic name and nutrition values from the pack label or a USDA record, plus an openly licensed Wikimedia Commons picture. Your photo and anything about you are never added.

Technical information

Like any website or online service, our hosting provider records standard request information (such as IP address, time, and the page or feature requested) to keep the service secure and working. We use the IP address briefly, in memory, to limit how many photos can be sent per hour. These logs are kept for up to 30 days and are not used to identify or profile you.

Stored on your device

The app keeps your own settings on your device, such as whether you agreed to the photo notice. Bowl changes are kept only while the app is open. A Nutrition Passport image is saved to your Photos only when you tap Save, and links are shared only when you choose to share them.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask what personal data we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing info@colourfuel.ie. Because we have no accounts and do not keep photos, we usually hold nothing about you beyond short-lived technical logs. You can also complain to the Irish Data Protection Commission (dataprotection.ie).

Information, not advice

Nutrition figures and photo estimates are for general information. They are not medical or dietary advice.

Changes

If we change how we handle information, we will update this page and its date.
