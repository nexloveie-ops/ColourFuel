ColourFuel Support

Need help or found something wrong? Email info@colourfuel.ie and tell us your iPhone model and what you were doing. We aim to reply within two working days.

How do I change a portion?

Tap any food around the bowl and drag the slider. Calories and nutrients update straight away. Tap Original, Protein+ or Active Day to switch options, and Reset to go back after adding foods.

Why isn't the sauce in the nutrition figures?

Sauce nutrition isn't included yet, so the figures cover the base ingredients only. It is never counted as zero.

How accurate is the photo estimate?

It identifies the foods and estimates their weights from the picture, so weights are guesses, not measurements. You can adjust every weight. Nutrition comes from published food records (USDA FoodData Central, UK CoFID).

What happens to my photo?

It is sent securely to our server and analysed by Claude (Anthropic) to identify foods, then discarded. ColourFuel does not store it. See our Privacy Policy.

A food from my photo was added to the library — what does that mean?

If a food isn't in our library yet, we add it using the pack label in your photo or a USDA record, with an openly licensed photo. It is marked "not yet reviewed" until our team checks it.

Is this medical advice?

No. ColourFuel gives general food information, not medical or dietary advice.
