## Version 1.0
**Promotional text (≤170)**
Pick a ColourFuel bowl, slide every portion to suit you and watch calories, protein and 30+ nutrients update live. Snap a meal for an instant estimate.

**Description**
ColourFuel turns a healthy bowl into something you can see and shape.

BUILD YOUR BOWL
• Choose from our colourful bowls and swipe between them
• Drag a slider to change any portion; calories, protein, carbs, fat and fibre update instantly
• Try Protein+ or Active Day, or add foods from our ingredient library
• Every change shows on the bowl (+50 g, New) and Reset takes you back

KNOW WHAT'S INSIDE
• Nutrition comes from published food records (USDA FoodData Central, UK CoFID), per 100 g and per portion
• Adult reference intakes are shown as label comparisons, not personal targets
• Sauce nutrition is shown separately and never counted as zero

INGREDIENT LIBRARY
• Browse 100+ foods by ingredient or by nutrient, with photos and sources
• See where a food ranks in our library for vitamin C, protein, fibre and more
• Find a food by photo; foods we don't have yet are added from the pack label or a USDA record

PHOTO ESTIMATE
• Snap a meal and get an estimate of the foods and weights, then adjust every weight
• Photos are analysed by Claude (Anthropic) to identify foods and are not stored by ColourFuel

NUTRITION PASSPORT
• Save a shareable card of your bowl to Photos or share a link

Estimates are for information only and are not medical or dietary advice.

## Notes for App Review
ColourFuel is a nutrition companion for our bowl menu and a general food library. No account or login is needed.
To try the photo estimate: tap the purple camera on the home screen (or the camera beside "Search foods" in the ingredient library), agree to the notice, and choose any food photo. Photos are analysed by Claude (Anthropic) to identify foods; nutrition is then calculated from published records. Ordering is not offered in the app.
